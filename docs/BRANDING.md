# Branding Guide

## App Name
**Sky Cube Runner 3D**

## Tagline (EN)
**Run the sky. Dodge the impossible.**

## الشعار (AR)
**اركض في السماء وتجاوز المستحيل.**

## Color Palette (Suggested)
- **Midnight Navy:** `#0B1220`
- **Neon Cyan:** `#4CC9F0`
- **Electric Pink:** `#F472B6`
- **Lava Orange:** `#F97316`
- **Soft White:** `#E6F1FF`

## Typography Suggestions
- **Primary (UI):** SF Pro / SF Pro Display
- **Secondary (Marketing):** SF Pro Rounded or Inter
- **Arabic Pairing:** IBM Plex Sans Arabic or Tajawal

## Logo/Key Visual Notes
- Use geometric cube motifs and neon trails.
- Favor clean gradients and high-contrast HUD elements.
- Avoid excessive texture; keep it premium and minimal.
